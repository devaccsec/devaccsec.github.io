Privacy policy

DOT Compliance Bot is a private tool of a trucking safety team. This page says what it does with data, including the access it is given to a Google account.

What the bot handles

Documents that team members send it in Telegram: roadside inspection reports and traffic tickets, which contain driver names, carrier names and numbers, and court dates; and a picture of each team member's own signature, which they keep in their folder in the team's Drive.

Google account access

The team's Google account authorises the bot to use its Google Drive and to send e-mail from its Gmail.

Drive: the bot creates folders, PDF files and spreadsheets inside the team's own folder, reads and updates those same files, and reads the spreadsheets the team keeps for its court cases and its attorney. It does not change or delete anything else in the Drive, and it does not share any of it with anyone.

Gmail: the bot only sends messages. When an inspection report has been signed, it e-mails that signed report to the address printed on it for its return. It does not read, search, change or delete any e-mail.

Who else sees the data

Pages are read by Google Gemini to extract their fields, and messages travel through Telegram. Carrier numbers may be looked up in FMCSA's public records. A signed inspection report goes to the agency whose address is printed on it. Nothing is sold or given to anyone else.

Keeping and removing data

Records are kept in the team's own Drive and on the server that runs the bot, for as long as the team needs them. The account owner can remove the bot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the files from Drive.

Contact

Questions go to the team member who invited you to the bot.
